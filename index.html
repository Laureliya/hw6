04 · Grammar

Comparatives without confusion

Use comparatives when you want to say that one product is different from another.

1. Short adjectives

small → smaller

light → lighter

cheap → cheaper

Для коротких прилагательных часто добавляем -er.

2. Ending in -y

easy → easier

heavy → heavier

-y меняется на -ier.

3. Longer adjectives

more comfortable

less expensive

more practical

Используем more / less перед прилагательным.

4. Irregular adjectives

good → better

bad → worse

Эти формы нужно запомнить.

Important difference

These headphones are a bit cheaper. — Эти наушники немного дешевле.

These headphones are much cheaper. — Эти наушники намного дешевле.

This pair is cheaper than the other one. — Эта пара дешевле другой.

07 · Reading practice

The wrong size

Read a new story about a problem in a clothes shop.

Last Saturday, I went shopping for a new jacket. I found a dark blue one that looked really nice. It was cheaper than the other jackets, so I decided to try it on.

Unfortunately, it was a bit too small. I asked the shop assistant, “Excuse me. Do you have one of these in a bigger size?” She checked the computer, but my size was out of stock.

Then she showed me another jacket. It was more expensive, but it was lighter and much more comfortable. It suited me better, too. I wasn't sure because I couldn't afford to spend much money.

The assistant explained that the jacket was on sale, so the price was reduced. In the end, I bought it. I'm happy with my choice because it's practical and I can wear it every day.

This is an additional practice story, not one of the original Outcomes listening conversations.
